[3] FDI — THE MAPPING HAS NO ESTABLISHED BASIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Box 1 avg 2018-19:  Total -3.1  =  Residents 0.1  +  Diaspora -3.2
  Table 5 net DI over IMF GDP, avg 2018-19 : -3.095%
  Table 5 DI LIABILITIES over IMF GDP      : +3.894%
  ours, equity EXCLUDING reinvested        : +3.203%
  ours, equity INCLUDING reinvested        : +3.634%

  THE SOURCE CONTRADICTS ITSELF. The footnote says the diaspora row is equity
  liabilities under FDI. But Box 1's Diaspora row (-3.2) is essentially the
  WHOLE of net direct investment (-3.095%), with Residents left at only +0.1%.
  Equity liabilities are +3.894% — nowhere near -3.2. The two readings
  cannot be reconciled from the published material.

  Our excluding-reinvested series averages 3.203%, which sits close to the
  3.2 the Box prints. That is a COINCIDENCE WITH A DIFFERENT CONCEPT, not
  evidence: the Box's own arithmetic identifies that row with net DI, which is
  a different quantity. The mapping is therefore NOT established, and the 2020
  test fails: see the line table below, where DI deviates by -1.26 pp.
  For this reason an FDI-EXCLUDED variant is carried alongside.

[6] BENCHMARK RESOLUTION FLOOR
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Box 1 prints to one decimal, so its rows are only resolved to +/-0.05 pp,
  which on avg 2018-19 GDP of 6915 EUR m is +/-3.5 EUR m.
  The travel line deviates by -0.04 pp. Any revision is therefore bounded
  at about 7.1 EUR m of travel credits ((|-0.04| + 0.05)/100 * 6915 / 0.92);
  it does NOT establish that travel was unrevised.
